Returns & Exchanges

We want you to be happy with your purchase. If something is not right, you can return or exchange it under the terms below.

1. Return Window. You can request a return or exchange within 3 days of receiving your order. Requests made after 3 days cannot be accepted.

2. Condition of Items. Items must be unworn, unwashed and undamaged, with all original tags and packaging attached. Items that show signs of use, wear, washing, perfume or alteration will not be accepted.

3. Items That Cannot Be Returned. Sale or discounted items, underwear, socks, and custom, personalised, made-to-order, manufacturing and bulk order products cannot be returned or exchanged unless they arrive faulty or incorrect.

4. How to Request a Return. Contact us within 3 days of delivery through the Contact page or on WhatsApp at +92 314 1385681 with your order number, the item you want to return or exchange, and the reason. We will confirm whether the item is eligible and share the return instructions.

5. Exchanges. Exchanges are subject to stock availability. If the size or colour you want is not available, we will offer a different item or a refund.

6. Refunds. Once we receive and inspect the returned item, we will let you know whether the refund is approved. Approved refunds are issued to your original payment method, or by bank transfer, Easypaisa or JazzCash for Cash on Delivery orders, usually within 7 working days of approval.

7. Delivery Charges. Original delivery charges are not refundable, and the customer pays the cost of returning the item, unless the item was faulty, damaged or not what you ordered.

8. Faulty or Incorrect Items. If you receive a faulty, damaged or incorrect item, contact us within 3 days of delivery with your order number and photos of the item. We will arrange a replacement or full refund, including delivery charges.

9. Refused or Undelivered Orders. Cash on Delivery orders that are refused at the door or cannot be delivered because of incorrect details are not eligible for returns, and we may decline future Cash on Delivery orders.

10. Contact. For any questions about returns or exchanges, contact us through the Contact page or on WhatsApp at +92 314 1385681.
