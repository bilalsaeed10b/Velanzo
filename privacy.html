Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Velanzo collects, uses and protects your personal information when you use this website.

1. Information We Collect. When you place an order, create an account, request a quotation or contact us, we collect the details you provide, such as your name, email address, phone number, delivery address, city and order details. We may also collect basic technical information, such as your IP address and browser type, to keep the website secure and working properly.

2. How We Use Your Information. We use your information to process and deliver orders, manage your account, respond to inquiries and quotation requests, provide customer support, prevent fraud and spam, and improve our website and services. If you sign up for updates, we may send you news and offers, and you can unsubscribe at any time.

3. Sharing Your Information. We do not sell your personal information. We share it only where needed to run our business, such as with courier companies to deliver your order, payment providers to process payments, and service providers that host our website and data. We may also share information when required by law.

4. Cookies and Local Storage. The website uses cookies and browser storage to keep you signed in, remember your shopping bag and preferences, and keep the site secure.

5. Data Security. We take reasonable steps to protect your information, including secure connections, access controls and limits on repeated requests. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Data Retention. We keep your information only as long as needed for the purposes described in this policy, or as required by law, for example for accounting and tax records.

7. Your Rights. You can view and update your account details at any time. You may ask us to access, correct or delete your personal information by contacting us. Some information may need to be kept for legal or order-related reasons.

8. Children. This website is not intended for children under 13 to use on their own. Purchases for children should be made by a parent or guardian.

9. Changes to This Policy. We may update this policy from time to time. The version published on this page applies.

10. Contact. For questions about this policy or your information, contact us through the Contact page or on WhatsApp at +92 314 1385681.
